Important Information

The amounts displayed are target amounts, from official Safaricom limit packages. Actual Fuliza limits are determined by Safaricom and its lending partners. Selecting an amount or contacting us guarantee approval or a limit increase.

How Fuliza Limits Are Assessed

  1. Initial customer eligibility assessment.
  2. M-PESA account usage and transaction history.
  3. Credit and repayment history.
  4. Review by the relevant lending partners.
