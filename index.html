Understanding Fuliza Limit Stages

Stage 1: Initial Assessment

Your M-PESA account activity and eligibility are assessed when determining your Fuliza limit.

Stage 2: Active M-PESA Usage

Consistent use of M-PESA and other Safaricom services contributes to your customer activity history.

Stage 3: Repayment History

Timely repayment of Fuliza and other credit facilities can contribute positively to your credit assessment.

Stage 4: Limit Review

The participating lending bank reviews your credit profile and determines whether your limit should be maintained, increased or adjusted.

Important: These are explanatory stages, not official Safaricom limit levels. There is no guaranteed progression or fixed amount at each stage. Only Safaricom and its lending partners determine individual Fuliza limits.

How to Request a Limit Review

Use the official M-PESA menu:

  1. Dial *334# on your Safaricom line.
  2. Select option 00 for Fuliza M-PESA.
  3. Choose Refresh My Limit if available.
  4. Follow the instructions provided.

A review request does not guarantee an increase.
